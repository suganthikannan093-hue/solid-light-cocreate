# Judge guide — SOLID LIGHT: CO-CREATE

Public URL: https://suganthikannan093-hue.github.io/solid-light-cocreate/

**Track: Gaming.** Special-award framing: **Best First Five Minutes**. The opening is a complete co-creation beat you can finish seated, with a mouse or with pinched hands: learn the loop, watch LUMA’s solid fail honestly, repair it yourself, and celebrate a crossing.

Physical Quest verification is still open. Desktop play and the official IWER Quest 3 emulator are the tested paths. On a headset-less browser, use Enter XR as the primary action or Preview XR (`?emulate`) for an immersive preview.

Chapters 3 and 4 (Light Garden, Last Spark) and the Workshop remixes deepen the same rule: two silhouettes, one validated solid. They are not required to judge the opening.

## How to play the opening

1. Open the URL. If you already have a save, choose **Continue** (mouse or pinch) or **New game**. Comfort and audio settings restore automatically.
2. Complete Chapter 1 with one closed loop around the guide.
3. Choose **Next level**. Trace the *short* ribbon first. Wait for the short, warm-tinted solid and the “LUMA’s bridge falls short” prompt.
4. Draw a *longer* closed loop that reaches B. Watch the crossing and the celebratory payoff.
5. Open **How LUMA thinks** for the last decision. In XR, inspect depth from the side; on desktop, right-drag to orbit.

## What LUMA actually is

LUMA is **not an LLM**. It is a bounded, deterministic local search over candidate side profiles (default budget: 24 candidates / 800 ms). It scores walkability, clearance, shelter and light cost, commits the best valid solid, or requests a human edit for things it cannot change — especially cyan length. It cannot see intent. An optional cloud suggestion, off by default, never replaces this local planner.

## Spatial necessity

The solid is the intersection of your front silhouette and LUMA’s side silhouette. Desktop keeps both boards facing you, with orbit as a fallback. In immersive mode the amber silhouette is moved to a true side view: a seated lean or head turn is enough; walking is not required.

## Saved progress and replay

Completed chapters, the current chapter, Workshop unlocks, fewest-stroke / star records, and comfort/audio settings persist in `localStorage` (ignored cleanly in private mode). After *The Last Spark*, a Workshop list of four remixes is visible. Returning players get Continue vs New game.

## Honest limits

No physical Quest comfort study is claimed here. The Best First Five Minutes pitch is about a reliable authored opening, not an unverified headset study. A lightweight `?debug` overlay reports frames per second and frame time on desktop or in a headset preview; Quest frame rate is unverified without a physical headset.
